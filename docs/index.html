Lira · Design System

Two products. Four surfaces.

Two products, four surfaces. Shared primitives (color, type, space, radius, shadow) power the three page-shaped themes; the clinical app has its own, because it is not a page. The switcher above changes what you see, not just its colour: only the sections belonging to the chosen surface are shown.

Lira for Women · mobile app

Daily support, warm and visual

Peach background, soft mint accents, illustrated character, rounded everything. Built for low-overwhelm daily use.

lira · core.json

Lira for Women · website

Premium marketing, same warmth

Larger type ladder, refined spacing, peach hero, mint accents. The warmth of the app, sized up for marketing.

lira-website · women-web.json

Lira for Professionals · marketing page

Clinical, restrained, credible

Cool off-white surfaces, sage as the lead accent, tighter radii, serif display for editorial weight. Persuades a clinician to sign up.

lira-website · pro-web.json

Lira for Professionals · clinical app

Dense, functional, worked in daily

Slate and violet on Tailwind's scales. Tables, status chips and notes, not heroes. Where a clinician spends her working day, so it is quiet on purpose.
